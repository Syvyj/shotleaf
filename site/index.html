Screenshots and clipboard,

Capture, mark up and share any part of the screen. Keep everything you copy, and paste it back in a keystroke. A native macOS app that keeps your data on your Mac.

macOS 15 Sequoia or later · Apple silicon

Screenshots

One shortcut, and a calm editor opens right where you need it.

Pick exactly what you mean

The screen freezes, so menus and hover states stay put. Drag an area, click a window, or press ↩ for the whole screen.

  • Pixel loupe, size and colour under the cursor
  • R repeats the last area, C copies a colour
  • Delay, pointer on or off, 1× size for sharp small images

Mark it up

Arrows, boxes, ovals, callouts, pen, highlighter, numbered steps, blur, spotlight, ruler and crop, each with its own key. Everything stays editable until you copy.

  • Backgrounds and padding for polished shots
  • Remove a background in one click
  • Text or image watermark in any corner
  • Copy ⌘C, save as PNG, JPEG, HEIC or PDF, share, pin on top, or drag into any app

Scrolling screenshots

Pick an area and scroll. Shotleaf stitches a long page together, and sticky headers and footers appear only once.

Text from any screenshot

Select an area and get its text, recognised on your Mac. Works with English, Ukrainian and other languages.

Watermark photos

Add a watermark to many photos at once. Full quality, metadata and colour profile kept, originals untouched.

Expiring links

Share a shot as a link that disappears after 1, 6 or 24 hours, on a server you run yourself.

Edit again

Reopen the last shot or an image from the clipboard, and find recent shots in the menu.

Clipboard

Everything you copy, one shortcut away: ⇧⌘V.

History that stays out of the way

The panel opens by the mouse. Type to search, then click or press ↩ to paste.

  • Text, links, colours, images and files, with text found inside images
  • Passwords from password managers are never kept
  • Leave out the apps you choose
  • Paste several items together, or one after another

Paste it better

⌘K cleans trackers from links, changes letter case, joins lines, tidies JSON, or saves the item as a snippet.

Snippets

Keep replies, signatures and details in folders. Type an abbreviation anywhere and it expands, or open them with ⇧⌘B.

  • Date, time, clipboard and cursor position placeholders
  • Questions asked before pasting, like a name

Private by design

On your Mac

History, snippets and shots are stored locally. Text recognition runs on device.

No account

No sign-up, no tracking, no analytics.

Native

Built in Swift for macOS. Light on memory, and it looks at home on your Mac.

Install

Shotleaf is not notarised by Apple yet, so macOS asks once before the first launch.

  1. Move Shotleaf to Applications and open it.
  2. When macOS says it can't check the app, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  3. Follow the setup guide: Shotleaf asks for Screen Recording (for screenshots) and Accessibility (for pasting).
