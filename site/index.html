Installation

On first launch, macOS asks you to confirm opening the app.

  1. Open the download and drag Shotleaf into Applications.
  2. If macOS won’t open it, go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  3. Turn on the two permissions Shotleaf asks for: one for screenshots and one for pasting text.
