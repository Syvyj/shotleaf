Privacy

Shotleaf has no account, no analytics and no ads. What it keeps stays on your Mac.

What stays on your Mac

  • Clipboard history: the last 200 items you copied; pictures are kept for 30 days. You can change both, pin items, exclude apps, pause it or turn it off in Settings.
  • Passwords that password managers mark as private are never saved. Text that looks like a card number or an access token is never saved to disk and is gone from the history after a minute.
  • Snippets and their pictures.
  • Recent screenshots: the last 10, so you can open them again. Clear them from the menu.
  • Your writing style, if you turn it on: a few of your own finished texts, so completions sound like you. “Forget” in Settings deletes them.

All of it is in ~/Library/Application Support/Shotleaf. Text recognition and text completion run on your Mac.

When Shotleaf goes online

  • Downloading the completion model from Hugging Face, once, when you ask for it.
  • Checking for a new version: at launch and once a day Shotleaf asks GitHub which release is the newest. Nothing about you is sent; turn it off in Settings › General.
  • A link to a screenshot, when you press the link button: the picture goes to Shotleaf's link service on Cloudflare (s.syvyj.dev) and is kept there only until the link expires, 12 hours at most. Or to your own server, if you set one up.

Nothing else: no usage statistics, no crash reports sent on their own.

Permissions

Screen Recording is for screenshots and text from the screen. Accessibility lets Shotleaf complete your text, expand snippets and paste from the history: it reads the field you type in, but doesn't read password fields.

This website

No cookies, no analytics, no third-party scripts; fonts are served from this site. It is hosted on Cloudflare, which handles the requests.
